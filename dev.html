ISLANDLEDGER · BUILD NOTES

Development

What currently works, what remains a prototype, and what needs further engineering.

WORKING

Current capabilities

  • Add and remove income and expense transactions.
  • Choose categories, dates and currencies.
  • View income, expenses, balance and recent activity.
  • Review monthly money flow and spending categories.
  • Create savings goals and manage budget plans.
  • Use responsive dashboard and theme controls.

DATA & PRIVACY

Local browser storage

Transactions, goals, settings and budget plans are stored in this browser. There is no account or cloud sync. Do not enter sensitive financial or banking information.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS

Not implemented

  • No bank/card connections, accounts or cloud backup.
  • Currency conversion relies on an external rate service and internet access.
  • Savings progress is manual; it does not move or reconcile money.
  • Budget plans do not automatically create transactions.
  • This is not a bank, accounting service or financial advice.

NEXT DEVELOPMENT

Planned work

  • Automated tests for totals, conversion and saved plans.
  • Review validation, storage failure handling and backup/export.
  • Complete keyboard, screen-reader and real-device testing.
  • Define security requirements before accounts or integrations.
